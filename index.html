VERIFIED FAULTS & RECORDS

Mileage correction

When an instrument cluster or vehicle module develops a fault, the displayed reading may no longer reflect the vehicle’s true distance travelled. Any correction must be based on reliable evidence and accurately documented.

  • Review of the vehicle and reported fault
  • Supporting mileage evidence and service history
  • Clear records of any authorised work

02!

COMPLIANCE-FIRST ENQUIRIES

Mileage blockers

Mileage-blocking technology can stop distance being recorded while a vehicle is in use, creating important road-use, insurance, vehicle-history and consumer-disclosure concerns. Every enquiry must be assessed against its intended application and current requirements.

  • No supply or fitting for public-road use
  • No assistance with concealing true mileage
  • No support for misleading buyers or records
Discuss a compliance question ↗

SPEED TECHS will not supply or fit mileage blockers for public-road use, mileage concealment or evasion of mileage-based charges. Any discussion is subject to a compliance review.
