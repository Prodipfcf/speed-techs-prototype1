# SPEED TECHS — Service-led Landing Page Prototype (v2)

A responsive single-page concept for SPEED TECHS, aimed at a Manchester (UK) vehicle technology audience. This iteration places the three services the client identified at the centre of the page: mileage correction, mileage blocker enquiries and vehicle remapping. Coding & programming remains an additional service.

## Preview locally

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The page uses the included local SVG brand artwork and custom vector vehicle illustration. No build step is required.

## What changed in v2

- Hero headline and first-screen content now explicitly feature Mileage Correction, Mileage Blockers and Vehicle Remapping.
- The three priority services are displayed as the main service cards.
- Added dedicated detail panels for each priority service with clear enquiry calls to action.
- Moved Coding & Programming into a secondary-service panel.
- Increased all explicitly styled text sizes below 14px to at least 14px; core body copy is generally 15–17px and form fields are 16px.
- Improved spacing and responsive behaviour for service cards, detailed service panels and mobile navigation.
- Updated page title, description and page copy to match the client’s service focus.

## Important content guardrails

- Mileage correction is described only for verified faults and evidence-supported true mileage, with records.
- Mileage blocker enquiries are framed as compliance-first only. The page does not promote public-road mileage suppression, concealment of true mileage or misleading buyers/records. Confirm the client’s exact intended service scope and legal advice before publishing.
- Vehicle remapping copy asks for vehicle-specific assessment and applicable emissions, roadworthiness and insurance requirements.
- No fabricated reviews, addresses, telephone numbers, customer claims, awards or performance guarantees are included.

## Before live launch

1. Confirm the final business name and legal business/contact details.
2. Replace the current WhatsApp placeholder with a verified business WhatsApp URL/number. The current floating button links to the quote section only.
3. Connect the quote form to an approved email inbox, CRM or WordPress form plugin. The current form does not send or store submissions.
4. Add genuine customer reviews only after receiving approved feedback.
5. Add a privacy notice and any appropriate cookie information. The checkbox is a prototype only, not a complete privacy solution.
6. Confirm all mileage-related service descriptions and permitted scope with the client and a qualified UK adviser before publishing.
7. Confirm real service areas, opening hours, contact details, policies, final images and legal disclosures.
